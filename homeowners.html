Construction support for homeowners

Understand your plans, costs and site quality before and during construction.

1. Design and Drawings

Suitable plans and drawings prepared through qualified professionals, based on your needs, plot and budget.

Typical deliverables

  • Floor plans
  • Drawing sets for discussion and approval

2. Detailed Cost Estimates

Item-wise quantities, rates and estimated costs so you know what the work should cost.

Typical deliverables

  • Item-wise estimate
  • Quantity and rate summary

3. Site Visits and Quality Review

Site observations and reports on work in progress, so issues are noted early.

Typical deliverables

  • Site observation report
  • List of points to correct

4. Material Consultation

Material comparisons and budget-based guidance to help you choose.

Typical deliverables

  • Material comparison
  • Budget-based recommendations

5. Budget Planning

Stage-wise construction budgets and expense planning.

Typical deliverables

  • Stage-wise budget
  • Expense plan

Exact deliverables, timeline and cost are confirmed in your written quotation before any work starts.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to share to get a quotation?

Your location, project stage, services needed, deadline, and any plans or documents you already have.

Can I choose only one service?

Yes. You can request a single service or a combination.

How is the cost decided?

You receive a quotation with scope and timeline. Work starts only after you approve it.

Do you build the house?

No. InfraDesk provides technical and management support. It is not a construction contractor.
